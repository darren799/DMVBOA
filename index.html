About DMVBOA

Come aboard for the ride.

DMVBOA is more than boating — it’s a growing community of boaters, adventurers, and water lovers from across the DMV area.

From raft-ups and cruises to live music, charity events, boating education, and unforgettable weekends on the water, DMVBOA brings people together through experiences that create lifelong memories.
